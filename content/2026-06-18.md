A study showed that mothers in the US make 11% less money than women without children and 42% less than fathers. This is called the “motherhood penalty.”

Many mothers said having kids hurt their jobs. Some stopped working because childcare was too expensive. Others had to work part-time. They lost money and job benefits. Some couldn’t save for retirement. One woman said she lost 750,000 dollars. Some mothers said men at work got more often higher positions. Others said their husbands made more money, and they had to stay home. Most mothers said they love their children and don’t regret being moms, but they didn’t know it would cost so much. They feel it is not fair.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**make less money** （赚得更少钱）
Used to describe earning a smaller amount of money compared to others or a previous time. Common in discussions about income and salary.
- Younger workers often make less money than their experienced colleagues.
- Due to the economic downturn, many businesses had to make less money last quarter.

**hurt their jobs** （损害他们的工作）
To negatively affect one's employment or career prospects. This phrase implies a damaging impact on their professional life.
- Frequent absences can hurt their jobs.
- Not having the right skills might hurt their jobs in the future.

**stay home** （待在家里）
To remain at one's residence, often used to describe not going out or, in this context, not working outside the home.
- When she was sick, she had to stay home from school.
- Many parents choose to stay home and raise their children.

**Q: What is the 'motherhood penalty'?**
**A (EN):** The 'motherhood penalty' is the term used to describe the phenomenon where mothers earn less money than women without children and significantly less than fathers.
**A (中):** “母亲惩罚”是指母亲比没有孩子的女性赚得少，比父亲赚得少得多的现象。

**Q: Why did some mothers stop working?**
**A (EN):** Some mothers stopped working because the cost of childcare was too high, and they also lost money and job benefits.
**A (中):** 一些母亲停止工作是因为托儿费用太高，而且她们也失去了金钱和工作福利。

**Q: Inferential: Based on the article, what might be a societal expectation that contributes to the 'motherhood penalty'?**
**A (EN):** An underlying societal expectation might be that mothers are primarily responsible for childcare and domestic duties. This expectation could lead employers to perceive mothers as less committed to their careers or to penalize them for taking time off or working fewer hours, thus contributing to lower pay and fewer promotions compared to fathers or childless women.
**A (中):** 一个潜在的社会期望可能是，母亲主要负责育儿和家务。这种期望可能导致雇主认为母亲对事业的投入较少，或者因为她们休假或工作时间较短而惩罚她们，从而导致与父亲或没有孩子的女性相比，薪水较低、晋升机会较少。
