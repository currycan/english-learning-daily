## 📖 文章 / Article

16-07-2026 15:00

Scientists study how cancer cells grow in space. The result surprises everyone. Cancer cells grow much faster than on Earth. Scientists believe that the reason is weak gravity.

Scientists focus on a gene called adar1. This gene makes cancer return. When scientists change this gene, they stop cancer cells from multiplying. A drug named Rebecsinib works against this gene. Astronauts help by giving their blood for testing. However, the drug is not ready for people yet. Scientists hope to start tests soon to use this new way to stop cancer.

Difficult words: cell (the smallest part of a living thing), gravity (the force which pulls things towards the Earth), multiply (to increase a lot in number).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**grow much faster** （生长得快得多）
Used to compare the speed of growth, especially when one thing grows significantly quicker than another.
- In the lab, the bacteria grew much faster than in the wild.
- The company's profits grew much faster this quarter.

**reason is** （原因是）
Used to introduce an explanation for a particular event or situation. Common and direct.
- The reason is that the weather was bad.
- I think the reason is too much homework.

**works against** （针对…起作用）
Describes something that is effective in opposing or preventing a particular condition or substance.
- This medicine works against the virus.
- The new policy works against illegal immigration.

**stop cancer** （阻止癌症）
Refers to methods or treatments aimed at preventing the development or spread of cancer.
- Scientists are trying to find new ways to stop cancer.
- Early detection can help stop cancer from spreading.
