## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**come at night** （晚上来）
Used to describe people arriving at a specific location during the nighttime. Generally neutral in register.

- Wild animals often come at night to search for food.

**need money** （需要钱）
Expresses a necessity or requirement for financial resources. Very common and neutral.
- Many women dance because they need money.
- He had to work two jobs because he needs money for his family.

**think badly of** （看不起，对...有坏看法）
To have a negative or critical opinion about someone or something. Often implies disapproval.
- Families and villages think badly of the girls.
- Some people think badly of those who don't follow the rules.

**pay to watch** （花钱观看）
To give money in exchange for the opportunity to see an event or performance. Common and straightforward.
