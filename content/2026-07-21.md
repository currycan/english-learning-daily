Scientists watch wild chimpanzees in Uganda. They eat special plants to help them feel better. These plants can stop pain and kill bacteria.

Researchers watch the chimps for four years. They follow the chimps and see what they eat when sick or hurt. They take those plants and test them. They think this can help find new medicines.

The study shows that many plants stop bacteria and help with pain. This means that chimps know which plants can help them. They are smart.

Difficult words: bacteria (a very small organism which can make you sick), pain (when something hurts), smart (clever).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**feel better** （感觉好些）
To improve one's health or mood after being sick or unhappy. Used in informal and general contexts.
- After resting, she started to feel better.
- This soup always makes me feel better when I have a cold.

**stop pain** （止痛）
To prevent or relieve physical suffering. Used in medical and general contexts.
- This medicine is very effective to stop pain.
- She hoped the massage would help stop pain in her back.

**kill bacteria** （杀死细菌）
To destroy harmful microorganisms. Used in medical and scientific contexts.
- Antiseptic wipes are used to kill bacteria on surfaces.
- Washing your hands helps to kill bacteria and prevent infections.

**help find** （帮助发现）
To assist in the process of discovering something new. Used in research and general contexts.
- This research could help find a cure for the disease.
- Her observations helped find the missing piece of the puzzle.
