The police try to close the buildings. It is not easy. Scammers have many buildings in many places. You must be careful.  Do not answer strange calls, messages, or friends online. It is a good way to stay safe.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**get calls, messages, or emails** （接到电话、消息或邮件）
This describes the action of receiving communication through different methods, often unsolicited in the context of scams.
- She often gets calls, messages, or emails from unknown numbers.
- Be cautious if you get calls, messages, or emails asking for personal information.

**wants their money** （想要他们的钱）
This phrase refers to someone's intention or desire to obtain money from another person, typically in a dishonest way when referring to scammers.
- The suspect wants their money by selling fake products.
- The advertisement clearly states the company wants their money upfront.

**stay safe** （保持安全）
This is a common phrase used to advise someone on how to protect themselves from danger or harm.
- Always lock your doors at night to stay safe.
- Traveling in groups is a good way to stay safe in unfamiliar cities.

**Q: What do scammers do to get people's money?**

**Q: Why is it difficult for the police to close down the scam centers?**
**A (EN):** It is difficult because scammers have many buildings in many different places.
**A (中):** 因为骗子在很多不同的地方都有很多建筑，所以警察很难关闭这些诈骗中心。
