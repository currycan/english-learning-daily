## 📖 文章 / Article

13-07-2026 15:00

Today’s world is full of technology. We look at screens a lot. We do not write in hand so much anymore. Handwriting seems less important. But is it?

Experts say that writing in hand is very important, especially for children. Handwriting helps the brain develop well. Neat handwriting can even help children get better grades in school. Writing helps children connect letters with sounds. It is different from typing on a computer.

Writing with a pencil and paper may be better for memory.  It helps improve thinking. So, handwriting is still important for children to learn and practice.

Difficult words: develop (to grow or change), neat (clean or smooth), improve (to make something better).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**full of technology** （充满技术）
Used to describe a world or environment that has many advanced tools and machines.
- Our cities are now full of technology, from smart traffic lights to digital billboards.
- This classroom is full of technology, with interactive whiteboards and individual tablets for each student.

**less important** （不那么重要）
Used to say that something has lost some of its value or significance compared to before or to something else.
- With the rise of streaming services, physical movie rentals are becoming less important.
- For younger generations, social media seems to be more important than traditional news outlets.

**connect letters with sounds** （将字母与声音联系起来）
Refers to the fundamental process in learning to read and write, where a child learns which letter symbols represent specific spoken sounds.
- Phonics exercises are designed to help children connect letters with sounds.
- This new reading program focuses on helping students connect letters with sounds more effectively.

**better for memory** （对记忆更好）
Suggests that a particular method or activity can help a person remember things more effectively.
- Studying flashcards can be better for memory than just rereading notes.
- Writing things down by hand is often said to be better for memory than typing them.
