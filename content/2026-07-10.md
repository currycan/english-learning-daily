## 📖 文章 / Article

07-09-2026 15:00

A report says that people keep more unused electronics. Four years ago, one family usually has 20 things. Now, it is about 30. Most people keep remote controls, old phones, and hairdryers.

People want to recycle these things. But it is not so easy. There are too many things. Companies are not able to recycle them all. It is not good. These things have important materials in them. We can use these materials again. It saves natural resources.

Another problem is that companies do not make good products. Some products do not last long. Also, it is not possible to repair them. People throw them away and buy new things. It is good for companies but not for the natural world.

Difficult words: unused (something what we do not use anymore), remote control (a device which we use to control the TV), natural resources (things such as minerals, wood, coal, gas, and so on).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**keep more unused electronics** （保留更多未使用的电子产品）
This phrase describes the habit of people holding onto electronic devices they no longer use.
- Many people confess to keeping more unused electronics than they need.
- The report highlights the trend of keeping more unused electronics in households.

**not able to recycle them all** （无法全部回收）
This means that the amount of waste is too large for the recycling systems to handle completely.
- Due to the sheer volume, the city is not able to recycle them all.
- The recycling plant is overwhelmed and not able to recycle them all.

**saves natural resources** （节约自然资源）
This means that by reusing materials or reducing consumption, we are protecting the Earth's valuable natural supplies.
- Recycling paper saves natural resources like trees.
- Using less water saves natural resources for the future.

**throw them away** （把它们扔掉）
This is a common idiom meaning to discard something as waste.
- When the phone broke, I had to throw them away.
- Don't just throw them away; see if they can be fixed.

## ❓ 理解 / Comprehension

**Q: Why is it difficult for companies to recycle all the electronic waste?**
**A (EN):** Companies are not able to recycle all the electronic waste because there are simply too many items to process.
**A (中):** 公司之所以无法回收所有电子垃圾，是因为需要处理的物品数量实在太多了。

**Q: What are the two main problems with electronics mentioned in the article?**
**A (EN):** The two main problems are that people keep too many unused electronics, and companies do not make products that last long or are easy to repair.
**A (中):** 文章提到的电子产品两大问题是：人们保留太多未使用的电子产品，以及公司生产的产品不耐用或不易修理。

**Q: What is the connection between throwing away products and the natural world?**
**A (EN):** Throwing away products, especially those that are not easily repaired, leads to more waste. This is bad for the natural world because it consumes natural resources and creates pollution, unlike when products are repaired or recycled, which helps preserve resources.
**A (中):** 丢弃产品（尤其是那些不易修复的产品）会导致更多垃圾。这对自然世界是不好的，因为它消耗自然资源并造成污染，不像修理或回收产品那样有助于保护资源。
