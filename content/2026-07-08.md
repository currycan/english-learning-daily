## 📖 文章 / Article

07-07-2026 07:00

Many people wonder why we have hair on our heads. Do we need hair?

We have hair for important reasons. Hair keeps our brain safe. It keeps us warm in cold weather. It also helps to cool us down when it is too hot outside. Hair keeps UV rays away from our skin. UV rays are not healthy for our skin. Hair can be like whiskers to warn us of something dangerous.

In the past, hair probably helps people choose the right partner. These days it is not so important. But we have hats. They can do the same job as hair.

Difficult words: brain (the organ in our head which controls all body), ray (a narrow line in which light streams), whisker (a long hair which grows from the face of many animals).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**wonder why** （想知道为什么）
Used to express curiosity or to ask about the reason for something.
- People often wonder why some animals sleep so much.
- She started to wonder why he had not called her back.

**keeps us warm** （保持我们温暖）
Describes the function of something in preventing heat loss.
- This thick coat keeps us warm in winter.
- The blankets are designed to keep us warm at night.

**cool us down** （使我们凉爽下来）
Describes the process of reducing body temperature when it is hot.
- Drinking cold water can help to cool us down after exercise.
- The fans in the room helped to cool us down.

**away from** （远离）
Indicates a separation or keeping distance from something.
- Please keep your children away from the road.
- He moved the vase away from the edge of the table.
