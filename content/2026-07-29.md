## 📖 文章 / Article

28-07-2026 15:00

The global debate on ultra-processed food (UPF) has raised concerns about its impact on health.

UPF generally includes foods with ingredients not used in home cooking, like ready meals, pot noodles, and even sliced bread. Studies suggest a link between UPF and poor health. Dr. Chris Van Tulleken, a doctor, scientist, and journalist, has been vocal about the dangers of UPF, especially after seeing its effects during his work in humanitarian emergencies. Tulleken shifted his focus from infectious diseases to the commercial determinants of health. He studied how big companies influence health, particularly through infant and adult nutrition.

UPF is industrially processed to maximize profit, and it often contains additives not found in home kitchens. While processed foods have been part of human diets for millennia, UPF is different and linked to health issues due to high levels of salt, fat, and sugar.

Difficult words: vocal (expressing opinions or feelings freely or loudly), determinant (a factor that influences something).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**raised concerns about** （引起了关于…的担忧）
Used to express that a particular topic or situation has made people worried or anxious about something specific.
- The new policy has raised concerns about job security.
- The recent weather changes have raised concerns about the upcoming harvest.

**a link between** （…之间的联系）
Used to indicate a connection or relationship between two or more things.
- Scientists are studying a link between diet and disease.
- There is a clear link between regular exercise and better mental health.

**shifted his focus from** （将他的重点从…转移到）
Describes a change in someone's main area of attention or interest, moving from one subject to another.
- After the project ended, she shifted her focus from marketing to product development.
- The company has shifted its focus from traditional retail to online sales.

**part of human diets** （人类饮食的一部分）
Refers to ingredients or types of food that are commonly eaten by people.
- Fruits and vegetables have always been an important part of human diets.
- Legumes are a fundamental part of human diets in many cultures.
