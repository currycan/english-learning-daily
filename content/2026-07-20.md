The technology in swimming is very advanced, especially in swimsuits.

Designers always try to improve them, making them better for swimmers. The Olympic swimsuits are very expensive and high-tech. The sports body has rules about what materials and designs athletes can use, but companies still try to make the best suits. Athletes test different suits to find the best one. For example, the new Arena suit is easy to put on. It takes only two minutes instead of the usual ten. It makes swimmers feel light in the water.

Testing in water shows that the suit helps reduce bubbles and helps athletes swim faster. But you must test a few more times before choosing your suit for the race.

Difficult words: advanced (modern and well-developed), designer (a person who creates new things), high-tech (using the most modern methods, designs, machines, and so on).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**try to improve** （试图改进）
Used to talk about efforts to make something better. Often used in contexts related to products, performance, or skills.
- The company will try to improve customer service next year.
- She is always trying to improve her English speaking.

**make them better** （使它们更好）
To cause something to become of higher quality or more effective. This is a general phrase used for improvement.
- The new software updates are designed to make the program better.
- Regular exercise can make your health better.

**easy to put on** （容易穿上）
Describes clothing or equipment that can be worn or applied quickly and without difficulty. Common for apparel.
- This jacket is easy to put on when you are in a hurry.
- The new helmet is very easy to put on.

**feel light** （感觉轻盈）
To have a sensation of being not heavy, often associated with speed, comfort, or a lack of burden. Used in sports or when describing clothing.
- After her run, she felt light and energized.
- The new shoes made her feet feel light.
