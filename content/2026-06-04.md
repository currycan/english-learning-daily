## 📖 文章 / Article

03-06-2026 15:00

Many people talk to AI chatbots. The chatbots help when people feel sad.

They are free and easy to use. People can talk to them any time they need. Some people do not have a therapist, so they use the chatbot. It can learn how people talk and give answers which help.

But the chatbot is not a real person. It does not feel or think. Some people need more help than a chatbot can give. A therapist is still very important. One doctor says we must use AI carefully.

Still, AI helps people with no one to talk to. Some people do not have money for a therapist or live far from one. Experts believe that more people will use chatbots in the future.

Difficult words: chatbot (a computer which talks like a person), therapist (a person who helps with bad feelings), carefully (with care).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**feel sad** （感到伤心）
Used to describe the emotional state of being unhappy. Common in everyday conversation.
- She started to feel sad when she heard the bad news.
- It's okay to feel sad sometimes.

**easy to use** （易于使用）
Describes something that is simple and convenient to operate or interact with. Often used for technology or tools.
- This new app is very easy to use.
- Online banking is designed to be easy to use for everyone.

**more help** （更多的帮助）
Indicates a greater level of assistance or support is needed. Can be used in various situations.
- She needed more help with her homework than her brother could provide.
- The project requires more help than we initially anticipated.

**in the future** （在未来）
Refers to a time that is yet to come. A common phrase for predictions or plans.
- Technology will continue to advance rapidly in the future.
- We hope to travel more in the future.
