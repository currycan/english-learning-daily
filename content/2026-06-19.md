## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**getting popular** （变得受欢迎）
To become liked by many people. Used in informal and general contexts.
- This new song is getting popular very quickly.
- Online courses are getting popular among students.

**make money from** （通过...赚钱）
To earn money by doing or selling something. A common business phrase.
- The company makes money from selling its software.
- She hopes to make money from her art exhibition.

**look real** （看起来真实）
To appear to be genuine or true. Used to describe appearances, especially in relation to art, photography, or simulations.
- The special effects in the movie look real.
- The artist's paintings look real, like photographs.

**working with** （与...合作）
To collaborate or cooperate with another person, company, or entity on a project or task. Common in business and project contexts.
- Our team is working with the marketing department on the new campaign.
- He is working with a famous chef to develop new recipes.
