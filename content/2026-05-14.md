## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**working on** （致力于，从事）
Used to describe the action of actively doing or developing something.
- The engineers are working on a new design for the car.
- She's working on her English fluency by practicing every day.

**put money into** （投资，投入资金）
To invest money in a project, business, or activity.
- Many investors are putting money into renewable energy projects.
- We decided to put money into renovating the old building.

**bring many new jobs** （带来许多新工作）
To create new employment opportunities.
- The new factory is expected to bring many new jobs to the town.
- Technological advancements can sometimes bring many new jobs in different sectors.

**because of** （因为，由于）
Used to state the reason for something.
- The flight was delayed because of bad weather.
- He succeeded because of his hard work and determination.
