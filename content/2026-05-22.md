Having unlimited vacation sounds great; however, it’s not.

More US companies move towards unlimited time off policies as it’s a popular thing to offer that helps with talent retention. It also shows trust in employees and it may be easier for the company’s administration.

However, unlimited PTO has potential pitfalls. It can fail if the company culture looks down on taking time off, and some employees might abuse the policy, which would put a burden on their co-workers and bring down productivity. Another issue to consider is that without restrictions, certain times of the year like summer and winter holidays, may bring lots of overlapping out-of-office messages.

Currently, around 6% of employees in the US offer unlimited time off, with Microsoft and Goldman Sachs being some of the most recent big-name companies that have announced it.

Difficult words: retention (the ability to keep or continue having something), PTO (personal time off), pitfall (an unexpected danger or difficulty).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**time off policies** （休假政策）
Refers to the rules and guidelines a company has regarding how employees can take time away from work.
- The company is reviewing its current time off policies to make them more flexible.
- Many employees are interested in companies with generous time off policies.

**talent retention** （人才保留）
The strategies and actions taken by a company to keep its employees from leaving.
- Competitive salaries and good working conditions are key for talent retention.
- Offering unlimited vacation is seen as a new approach to talent retention.

**looks down on** （看不起；瞧不起）
To disapprove of or have a low opinion of someone or something. This is often used when talking about company culture.
- If the company culture looks down on taking time off, the policy won't work.
- Some people look down on professions that don't require a university degree.

**put a burden on** （给...增加负担）
To cause someone or something to have difficulty or worry, often by giving them too much work or responsibility.
- Excessive overtime can put a burden on employees' health.
- Please don't put a burden on your colleagues by not finishing your tasks.

## ❓ 理解 / Comprehension

**Q: Why do some US companies offer unlimited time off?**
**A (EN):** Companies offer unlimited time off as a popular benefit to help retain talent, show trust in employees, and potentially simplify administration.
**A (中):** 公司提供无限假期作为一项受欢迎的福利，以帮助留住人才，展示对员工的信任，并可能简化管理。

**Q: What are some potential problems with unlimited vacation policies?**
**A (EN):** Potential problems include a company culture that discourages taking time off, employees abusing the policy, which burdens co-workers and reduces productivity, and an increase in overlapping out-of-office messages during busy periods.
**A (中):** 潜在的问题包括公司文化不鼓励休假，员工滥用政策，给同事带来负担并降低生产力，以及在繁忙时期重叠的“外出办公”消息增多。

**Q: Infer: If a company has a culture where employees are constantly pressured to work long hours and rarely take breaks, what is likely to happen if they introduce an unlimited vacation policy?**
**A (EN):** If the company culture discourages taking time off or puts pressure on employees to always be working, then even with an unlimited vacation policy, employees will likely still feel unable to take much time off due to fear of negative consequences or judgment from management and colleagues.
**A (中):** 如果公司文化不鼓励休假或给员工造成持续工作的压力，那么即使有无限假期政策，员工很可能因为害怕负面后果或管理层和同事的评判而仍然觉得无法休太多假。
