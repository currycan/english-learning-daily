## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**unusual turtle** （不寻常的乌龟）
Describes a turtle that is not typical or common. Used in a neutral to slightly surprising tone.
- The zoo had an unusual turtle with bright blue spots.
- Finding an unusual turtle in your backyard is quite rare.

**strong bite** （强有力的咬合）
Refers to the power of an animal's jaws when it bites. Often used to describe dangerous or powerful animals.
- Be careful, that dog has a very strong bite.
- The crocodile is known for its incredibly strong bite.

**hard to care for** （难以照顾）
Means that something requires a lot of effort, attention, or special knowledge to look after. Used for pets, plants, or even situations.
- Exotic pets can be very beautiful, but they are often hard to care for.
- This old car is fun to drive, but it's hard to care for.

**take care of** （照顾）
To provide for the needs of someone or something; to look after them. A very common and essential phrase for responsibility.
- It's our responsibility to take care of the environment.
- She decided to take care of the stray cat she found.
