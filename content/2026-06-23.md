## 📖 文章 / Article

22-06-2026 15:00

El Niño starts in the Pacific Ocean. This natural event makes the ocean water very warm. It changes the wind and rain around the whole world.

Scientists think that this El Niño will be stronger than ever. Together with climate change, it will make next year very hot. Some countries, like Australia and parts of Africa, will have very dry and hot weather. Other places will have big storms.

The United Nations says that governments must prepare right now. They need to help farmers grow food and make cool places to save people from dangerous heat.

Difficult words: natural (something what comes from or happens in nature and that people do not do), prepare (to get ready for something), heat (when it is too hot).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**around the whole world** （全世界）
This phrase means affecting or happening in every part of the Earth. It is commonly used.
- The news spread around the whole world very quickly.
- This new technology is changing lives around the whole world.

**climate change** （气候变化）
This refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns, usually caused by human activities. It is a very common and important term.
- Scientists are worried about the effects of climate change.
- Many countries are working together to address climate change.

**very dry and hot weather** （非常干燥炎热的天气）
A descriptive phrase used to indicate extreme weather conditions characterized by a lack of rain and high temperatures. Often used in news reports.
- The region has been experiencing very dry and hot weather for months.
- Farmers are struggling with the very dry and hot weather this season.

**prepare right now** （立即准备）
An urgent call to action, emphasizing the need for immediate preparation. Used in situations requiring prompt response.
- The authorities warned citizens to prepare right now for the approaching storm.
- It's crucial that we prepare right now for the upcoming exam.

## ❓ 理解 / Comprehension

**Q: What is El Niño?**
**A (EN):** El Niño is a natural event that starts in the Pacific Ocean and makes the ocean water very warm, changing wind and rain around the world.
**A (中):** 厄尔尼诺是一种自然现象，发生在太平洋，使海水变暖，从而改变世界各地的风雨。

**Q: According to the article, which areas are expected to have dry and hot weather?**
**A (EN):** Australia and parts of Africa are expected to have very dry and hot weather.
**A (中):** 澳大利亚和非洲部分地区预计将出现非常干燥炎热的天气。

**Q: Why does the United Nations say governments must prepare right now?**
**A (EN):** The United Nations says governments must prepare right now because scientists think this El Niño will be stronger than ever and, together with climate change, will make next year very hot, leading to dangerous conditions like extreme heat and big storms.
**A (中):** 联合国之所以说各国政府必须立即做好准备，是因为科学家们认为这次厄尔尼诺将比以往任何时候都要强，并且与气候变化一起，将导致明年异常炎热，从而带来极端高温和巨大风暴等危险状况。

**Q: What might be the connection between the stronger El Niño and the advice to help farmers and create cool places?**
**A (EN):** The stronger El Niño is predicted to cause very hot weather and potentially extreme heat. Helping farmers grow food is important because dry and hot weather can damage crops. Creating cool places is necessary to protect people from the dangerous heat, which can be life-threatening.
**A (中):** 更强的厄尔尼诺预计会导致炎热的天气，甚至极端高温。帮助农民种植粮食很重要，因为干燥炎热的天气会损害庄稼。建造凉爽的地方是为了保护人们免受危险高温的侵害，这种高温可能是致命的。
