## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**stopped his plan** （停止了他的计划）
Used when someone decides not to continue with something they had intended to do. Neutral register.
- The government stopped its plan to build a new highway due to public opposition.
- She stopped her plan to move abroad after getting a promotion.

**spoke out against** （公开发表反对意见）
To express disapproval or opposition publicly. Often used in formal or semi-formal contexts when discussing social or political issues.
- Many citizens spoke out against the new tax law.
- The organization spoke out against the company's unfair labor practices.

**walked away from** （放弃（交易、关系等））
To decide to stop being involved in a situation or deal, often because it is not going well or is too difficult. Can be used in business or personal contexts.
- The buyer walked away from the deal when they saw the poor condition of the house.
- After a long argument, he walked away from the friendship.
