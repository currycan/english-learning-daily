## 📖 文章 / Article

10-08-2026 15:00

The Panama Canal connects two oceans, the Atlantic and the Pacific Ocean. In 1913, workers must flood Lake Gatun to make the canal.

The canal is very important for global trade. Big ships go through the canal. The canal makes the way much shorter. But there is a problem – not enough rain. This year, the canal has very low water. You can even see trees in the water. It is not usual. This makes it hard for ships to pass. They have to go around Africa or Egypt. It takes more time and money.

This problem will get worse because Central America will have even less rain in the future. Experts do not know how to solve it or if it is even possible.

Difficult words: canal (a long thin stretch of water for boats and ships to travel along), flood (to cover with water), global trade (when countries do business together).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**connects two oceans** （连接两个海洋）
Used to describe a geographical feature or man-made structure that links two bodies of water.
- The Suez Canal connects the Mediterranean Sea and the Red Sea.
- This bridge connects the two sides of the river, making travel much easier.

**very important for** （对...非常重要）
Emphasizes the high level of significance or necessity of something for a particular purpose or entity.
- Regular exercise is very important for maintaining good health.
- Clear communication is very important for team success.

**not enough rain** （雨水不足）
Indicates a lack of sufficient rainfall, often leading to drought conditions or water shortages.
- The crops are dying because there has been not enough rain this season.
- The region is experiencing a severe water crisis due to not enough rain.

**get worse** （变得更糟）
Means that a situation or condition is deteriorating and becoming more severe or unpleasant.
- If we don't fix the leaky pipe, the damage will get worse.
- The traffic congestion tends to get worse during rush hour.
