You find a magic potion. If you drink it, you will live forever. You cannot get sick, and your body will stay the same age.

Some people think that this is very good. You can do many things, travel, and still learn something new. You never need to be afraid of death. But some people think that it is not good. Bernard Williams says that life can get boring if you repeat the same things for many years. Samuel Scheffler says that life is special because it is short. When we know that time is not forever, we choose what is important. With no end, life may lose meaning. Living forever can be both happy and sad.

Difficult words: magic (special power which changes things in a way which we cannot explain), potion (a drink with special power, often in stories), meaning (important quality why we do things).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**live forever** （永远活着）
To continue living for all future time; never die. This is a common phrase in fantasy and philosophical discussions.
- The fairy tale prince wanted to live forever with his true love.
- Scientists are exploring ways to extend human life, but the idea to live forever is still science fiction.

**stay the same age** （保持同一个年龄）
To not get older; to remain at a particular age. Used to describe an unchanging physical state over time.
- If I had a magic potion, I would want my body to stay the same age.
- In the movie, the character drank a potion that made her stay the same age for centuries.

**afraid of death** （害怕死亡）
To feel fear or worry about the end of one's life. A common human emotion that the potion would eliminate.
- The promise of eternal life means you would never be afraid of death.
- Many people spend their lives trying to come to terms with being afraid of death.

**lose meaning** （失去意义）
To no longer have a purpose or important quality. This happens when something becomes pointless or unremarkable.
- If life has no end, some people worry it might lose meaning.
- Without challenges, even the most exciting job can start to lose meaning.

**Q: What two benefits of drinking the magic potion are mentioned in the article?**

**Q: According to Bernard Williams, why might living forever not be a good thing?**
**A (EN):** Bernard Williams believes that life can become boring if you keep repeating the same activities over many years.
**A (中):** 根据伯纳德·威廉姆斯的说法，如果你多年来重复做同样的事情，生活可能会变得无聊。

**Q: Why does Samuel Scheffler think life is special?**
**A (EN):** Samuel Scheffler thinks life is special because it is short. Knowing that our time is limited encourages us to choose what is important.
**A (中):** 塞缪尔·谢弗勒认为生命之所以特别，是因为生命是短暂的。知道我们的时间是有限的，会促使我们选择重要的事情。

**Q: Inferential question: If someone lived forever, what might be a hidden challenge not explicitly mentioned in the text?**
**A (EN):** A hidden challenge could be the eventual loss of personal connections. As people around them die, an immortal individual might face profound loneliness and grief, having to witness the passing of all loved ones repeatedly over an endless existence.
**A (中):** 一个潜在的挑战可能是最终失去人际关系。随着身边的人死去，一个永生的人可能会面临深刻的孤独和悲伤，在永恒的存在中一次又一次地目睹所有亲人的离去。
