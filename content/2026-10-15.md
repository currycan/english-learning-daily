Jinwar is a village in northeast Syria. It is a safe place for women.

Workers build the village in 2018. The village has 30 houses. Women and children live there together. There are 20 families. The village has a school, a training academy, and a bakery. Women grow crops in fields. No men can live in Jinwar, but they can visit their families. Boys can live with their mothers until they get married.

When there is a war, women and children always suffer the most. Women in Jinwar work during the day and sing and dance at night. They have their own goals and dreams. Nobody controls them. They become stronger. They do not just do chores at home anymore.

Difficult words: crop (a plant which a farmer grows), suffer (to have a hard time), chore (a job which someone must do at home).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**safe place for women** （女性的安全之地）
This phrase indicates a location that provides security and protection for women.
- The shelter is a safe place for women escaping abuse.
- She searched for a safe place for women and children during the crisis.

**live there together** （一起住在那儿）
This describes people sharing a living space in a particular location.
- Many students live there together in the dormitory.
- The siblings decided to live there together after college.

**suffer the most** （遭受最大的痛苦）
This means to experience the worst or most severe difficulties or pain.
- During natural disasters, the elderly often suffer the most.
- In times of economic hardship, low-income families suffer the most.

**own goals and dreams** （自己的目标和梦想）
This refers to personal aspirations and ambitions that individuals wish to achieve.
- She encouraged her students to pursue their own goals and dreams.
- He was determined to achieve his own goals and dreams, regardless of others' opinions.
