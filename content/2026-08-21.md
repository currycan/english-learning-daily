Many US teenagers do not get enough sleep. Doctors say that teens need 8 to 10 hours, but most do not get so much. The main reason is that many schools begin lessons too early.

Experts say that schools must start later, after 8:30 am. When schools do this, students sleep more. They get better grades, and they feel happier. The community is safer because there are fewer car accidents. This is the right thing to do for all students.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**grow up** （长大）
To develop from a child into an adult. This is a common phrasal verb used in everyday conversation.
- Children grow up so quickly these days.
- What do you want to be when you grow up?

**body clock** （生物钟）
A natural internal rhythm that controls sleep and wakefulness. It's a specific term related to biology and health.
- Jet lag happens when your body clock is out of sync with the new time zone.
- Regular sleep schedules help to regulate your body clock.

**feel sleepy** （感到困倦）
To have the sensation of wanting to sleep. This is a very common and natural expression.
- After the long hike, I started to feel sleepy.
- The warm room made me feel sleepy.

**start later** （晚点开始）
To begin an activity, event, or school day at a later time than usual. This is a practical suggestion.
- The meeting will start later today because the boss is delayed.
- Many parents agree that schools should start later in the morning.
