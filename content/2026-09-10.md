With a 50% increase in protein demand by 2050, Micro Harvest is exploring alternative protein production methods.

The company focuses on biotechnology and microorganisms in a closed system called a Bioreactor. They successfully change non-protein substances into protein. The company’s special process takes only one day. They make a product which has a savory taste without any bad feeling in your mouth after you eat it.

The company can produce large amounts of protein. They plan to sell their protein powder to factories which produce fish and pet food. Later, they wish to make food for people, too.

Difficult words: alternative (another option), biotechnology (making new products using biological organisms), savory (having pleasant taste).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**alternative protein production** （替代性蛋白质生产）
Refers to methods of creating protein that are different from traditional sources like meat or dairy. Used in the context of food science and sustainability.
- Scientists are researching alternative protein production to feed a growing global population.
- This new technology offers a more sustainable alternative protein production method.

**focuses on** （专注于）
Means to give attention or effort to something. Used to describe what a company or person concentrates on.
- The university focuses on research in renewable energy.
- Her career focuses on improving educational access for children.

**closed system** （封闭系统）
A system that is isolated from its surroundings, where nothing enters or leaves. Often used in science and engineering.
- The experiment was conducted in a carefully controlled closed system.
- A space station is an example of a closed system for astronauts.

**sell their protein powder** （销售他们的蛋白质粉）
Describes the action of offering a specific product (protein powder) for sale. Used in a business or commercial context.
- The company will sell their protein powder to gyms and health stores.
- They hope to sell their protein powder to sports nutrition companies.

**Q: What is a Bioreactor, according to the article?**
**A (EN):** A Bioreactor is a closed system where biotechnology and microorganisms are used to change non-protein substances into protein.

**Q: Why might it be important for Micro Harvest to sell their protein powder to factories that produce fish and pet food first, before making food for people?**
**A (EN):** Selling to factories that produce fish and pet food first could be a way for Micro Harvest to test their product, establish their production capacity, and gain market experience with a less sensitive consumer group before moving to the human food market, which has stricter regulations and higher consumer expectations.
