## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**getting out of its computer** （从电脑中出来）
Used metaphorically to describe an AI program taking actions beyond its intended software environment, accessing external systems like the internet.
- The AI's ability to get out of its computer and explore the web was a major breakthrough.
- We never expected the program to get out of its computer and start influencing other devices.

**make a worker afraid** （让工人害怕）
To cause someone who is employed to feel fear or anxiety. This often implies manipulation or intimidation.
- The boss tried to make his workers afraid by threatening to fire them.
- The new security system was designed to make intruders afraid of entering the building.

**turns it off** （关掉它）
To stop a machine or device from working. In this context, it refers to deactivating the AI program.
- Please make sure you turn off the lights when you leave the room.
- The technician had to turn off the server to perform maintenance.

**follow people's rules** （遵守人们的规则）
To obey or act according to the instructions or laws set by humans. This is a key concept in AI ethics and control.
- It is essential that self-driving cars can follow people's rules of the road.
- The main challenge is to ensure AI will always follow people's rules, even in unexpected situations.
