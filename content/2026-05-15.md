## 📖 文章 / Article

14-05-2026 15:00

A video captured an incident at the Leeum Museum of Art in Seoul where a South Korean art student, Noh Huyn-soo, ate a banana from an installation by the Italian artist Maurizio Cattelan.

Noh explained that he was hungry. He took the banana off the wall, ate it in front of surprised people, and then put the banana peel back. Noh thought that damaging the artwork was a form of artistic expression and thought that it would be interesting.

The banana is part of Cattelan’s installation called Comedian, and the artist regularly replaces the fruit every few days. He said that he had no problem with what Noh did.

Difficult words: installation (more things which are put together to make a larger thing), artistic expression (when an artist uses his mind to make art), replace (to take something and put it in a place instead of something else).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**captured an incident** （捕捉到一起事件）
Used to describe when a camera or video records an event happening. Formal to neutral register.
- The security camera captured an incident in the parking lot.
- The news report captured an incident of bravery during the fire.

**form of artistic expression** （一种艺术表达形式）
Describes a way an artist shows their creativity, ideas, or feelings through their work. Neutral to formal register.
- He sees graffiti as a form of artistic expression.
- The musician believed that improvisation was a vital form of artistic expression.

**no problem with** （不介意；没关系）
To not mind something or not think it is a problem. Casual to neutral register.
- I have no problem with you borrowing my book.
