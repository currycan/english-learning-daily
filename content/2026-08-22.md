## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**body clock** （生理时钟）
The natural internal system that regulates sleep-wake cycles and other biological rhythms. It's a common term related to sleep science.
- My body clock is usually set to wake me up early, even on weekends.
- Jet lag happens when your body clock is out of sync with the new time zone.

**grow up** （成长）
To develop from a child into an adult. A common phrasal verb used to describe the process of maturation.
- Children grow up so quickly, it feels like yesterday they were babies.
- She hopes to grow up and become a doctor.

**feel sleepy** （感到困倦）
To experience the sensation of needing to sleep. A very common and natural way to describe tiredness.
- After a long day of studying, I start to feel sleepy.
- The warm weather made him feel sleepy.

**start later** （晚点开始）
To begin an activity or event at a later time than usual or planned. Often used in discussions about schedules and timing.
- The movie will start later than advertised due to technical issues.
- Many parents believe schools should start later in the morning.
