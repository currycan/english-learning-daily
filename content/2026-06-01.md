## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**make learning easier** （让学习更容易）
This phrase means to create conditions or add features that help people learn something with less difficulty.
- The new software is designed to make learning grammar easier for students.
- Teachers are looking for ways to make learning a new language easier for their students.

**step by step** （一步一步地）
This idiom means to do something gradually, in a series of small, manageable actions. It's often used to explain a process or instructions.
- The instructions explained how to assemble the furniture step by step.
- She learned to cook by following recipes step by step.

**learning progress** （学习进展）
This refers to how well a student is advancing in their studies and acquiring new knowledge or skills over time.
- Parents often want to know about their child's learning progress at school.
- Regular assessments help track a student's learning progress.

**improve their English** （提高他们的英语）
This phrase means to become better at speaking, understanding, reading, or writing English.
- He joined a conversation club to improve his English.
- Many students use apps and websites to improve their English skills.
