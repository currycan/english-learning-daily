## 📖 文章 / Article

21-07-2026 15:00

In Germany, some children, called ‘Roly Kids’  learn to use wheelchairs at a skate park.

They do things like wheelies. They learn to get up if they fall. A man named Matthias Wagner helps them. He uses a wheelchair too. He knows very well that it can be hard at first. He says that when people get their first wheelchair, they often feel confused. They do not know how to do many things. The Roly Kids meet every week to practice and have fun. Matthias tells parents not to worry too much. Children need to learn to do things on their own. The children learn to be strong and independent.

Difficult words: wheelie (a trick on a bike when you raise the front wheel off the ground and keep it in the air), confused (when you do not understand something), independent (when you can do things alone without help).

> "They do things like wheelies."

**practice** (verb) /ˈpræk.tɪs/ （练习）— to do something regularly to improve your skill or knowledge.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**learn to use** （学会使用）
Used to describe acquiring the ability to operate or utilize something.
- Children learn to use computers at a young age.
- She is learning to use new software for her job.

**get up if they fall** （摔倒后能站起来）
Describes the action of recovering one's balance and standing after an accidental fall. It implies resilience and the ability to overcome a setback.
- It's important for toddlers to learn to get up if they fall.
- The training teaches you how to get up if they fall on the slippery ice.

**know very well** （非常清楚，非常了解）
Expresses a deep or thorough understanding of a situation or feeling.
- I know very well how difficult it is to start a new business.
- She knows very well that honesty is the best policy.

**on their own** （依靠自己，独立地）
Means doing something by oneself, without help from others. It emphasizes self-reliance.
- At 16, he wanted to live on his own.
- She likes to figure out puzzles on her own.
