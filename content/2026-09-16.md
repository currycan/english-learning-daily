## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**world’s biggest event** （世界上最大的活动）
Used to describe the largest or most important occasion of its kind globally. It emphasizes scale and significance.
- The Olympics is the world's biggest event for athletes.
- This fashion show is the world's biggest event for designers.

**big news** （大新闻）
Important or exciting information that is likely to be of interest to many people. Often used in journalism and announcements.
- The company announced big news about a new product launch.
- We're waiting for big news from the election.

**make games cheaper** （让游戏更便宜）
To reduce the price of video games, making them more affordable for consumers. This is often a marketing or business strategy.
- The store is trying to make games cheaper during the holiday sale.
- Xbox wants to make games cheaper for its subscribers.

**coming back** （回归，回来）
To return after a period of absence or to reappear in a new form or context. Can refer to people, ideas, or even old trends.
- The singer is coming back after a long break.
- Retro fashion is coming back in style.

**look like real life** （看起来像真实生活）
To be so realistic in appearance or detail that it is difficult to distinguish from actual reality. Often used to describe advanced graphics or simulations.
- The new virtual reality headset makes the game look like real life.
- The special effects in the movie looked like real life.
