A famous psychologist gives us three rules to make children safe with technologies. People need other people to feel happy and safe.

Many years ago, children start using smartphones. They do not play outside with their friends. This is a big problem because children need to talk, have fun, and walk together. Because they are always online, many young people feel very lonely and sad. They watch too many short videos. They cannot look at a book or watch a long film.

These things can save our children. First, do not let children use social media before they are 16 years old. Second, schools should use real books and pens, not computers and tablets. Children learn from people, not from screens. Also, do not give children AI toys or robots which talk like friends. These machines do not care about children. They can hurt the love between a child and a parent.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**make children safe** （让孩子们安全）
To protect children from danger or harm. This is a general phrase used to describe actions taken for children's well-being.
- Parents try to make children safe by teaching them about traffic rules.
- The new laws are designed to make children safe from online predators.

**play outside** （在外面玩）
To engage in recreational activities in an outdoor space. This is a common phrase for physical activity and interaction with nature.
- Children need to play outside to get fresh air and exercise.
- In the summer, we often play outside until it gets dark.

**feel very lonely** （感到非常孤独）
To experience a strong sense of sadness or isolation due to a lack of connection with others. It's a common feeling, especially among young people.
- After moving to a new city, she started to feel very lonely.
- Sometimes, even when surrounded by people, you can still feel very lonely.

**talk like friends** （像朋友一样说话）
To communicate in a way that is friendly, informal, and shows affection or understanding, similar to how friends interact.
- The new AI assistant is programmed to talk like friends to its users.
- He tries to talk like friends with his colleagues to build a good team atmosphere.
