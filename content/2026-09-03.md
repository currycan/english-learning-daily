In 1848, construction worker Phineas Gage has a bad accident. An iron rod goes through his head and hurts the front part of his brain. Gage does not die, but his personality changes. He is not kind and organized anymore.

This event is important for doctors. It shows that the brain and personality are connected to some parts of the brain. Gage later finds work in Chile. His job is repetitive. For Gage, it is a therapy. His brain must learn to work differently.  Doctors learn that the brain can change.

Gage’s accident starts a new field of neuroscience. It teaches us that the brain can adapt and recover from serious injuries.

Difficult words: rod (a thin, straight, solid bar), brain (the organ inside the head), personality (how you act and what you think), repetitive (when something happens again and again).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**goes through his head** （穿过他的头）
Used to describe something physically passing through the head, often due to an accident.
- The bullet went through his head, but thankfully he survived.
- The sharp object went through his head during the accident.

**changes his personality** （改变了他的性格）
Refers to a significant alteration in someone's typical behavior, thoughts, and feelings.
- The illness sadly changed his personality, making him withdrawn.
- After the traumatic event, her personality changed dramatically.

**learn that** （了解到）
Indicates gaining knowledge or understanding about a particular fact or situation.
- Scientists learn that plants need sunlight to grow.
- We can learn that practice makes perfect.

**heal itself** （自我愈合）
The ability of the body or mind to recover from damage or illness without external medical intervention.
- The skin has a natural ability to heal itself.
- Many believe that a good rest can help the body heal itself.
