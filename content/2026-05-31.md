## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**step by step** （一步一步地）
Used to describe a process that is done gradually and in a logical order. It suggests a careful and methodical approach.
- The teacher showed the students how to solve the problem step by step.
- Learning a new language takes time and practice, step by step.

**understand how** （理解如何）
This phrase is used when someone wants to learn the way something works or how to do something.
- It's important to understand how the machine operates before using it.
- She read the instructions carefully to understand how to assemble the furniture.

**learning experience** （学习体验）
Refers to the overall feeling or process a student has while engaging with educational material or activities.
- The new interactive software aims to provide a more engaging learning experience.
- Teachers are always looking for ways to improve the student learning experience.

**improve their English** （提高他们的英语）
This phrase describes the action of making one's English language skills better.
- Many students use apps to improve their English in their free time.
- She joined a conversation club to improve her English speaking.
