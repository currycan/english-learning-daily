Many people learn English for years, but they still can’t speak it well.

Many students want a better way to learn English. These five simple rules can help.

One, learn new words in a story. Don’t learn only single words. A story helps you remember new words.

Two, read and listen to the same words again and again. Stop only when you know them well.

Three, speak English as much as you can. Reading and listening are good, but you learn more when you speak.

Four, use lessons for your level. If the lessons are very easy or very difficult, you learn very little.

Finally, have a plan. Follow your plan every day.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**learn English** （学习英语）
This is a common collocation used when talking about the process of acquiring English language skills. It is suitable for general conversation and academic contexts.
- She wants to learn English before she moves abroad.
- Many people find it challenging but rewarding to learn English.

**speak it well** （说得好）
This phrase describes the ability to use a language fluently and accurately in spoken form. It's commonly used to assess language proficiency.
- He's been studying for years and now he can speak it well.
- The goal of this course is to help students speak their new language well.

**as much as you can** （尽你所能地；尽可能多地）
This phrase is used to encourage someone to do something to the greatest extent possible. It's common in instructions and motivational advice.
- Practice speaking English as much as you can.
- Try to drink water as much as you can, especially on hot days.

**learn very little** （学到很少）
This phrase indicates minimal learning or understanding has occurred, often due to inappropriate difficulty level or ineffective methods.
- If the book is too advanced, you will learn very little.
- He felt he would learn very little from that lecture because it was too basic.
