## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**stop early** （提前结束）
Used to describe an event or activity that finishes before the planned or expected time.
- Due to bad weather, the outdoor concert had to stop early.
- The movie was so boring that many people left and the director decided to stop early.

**run onto the pitch** （跑进场内）
A phrase describing people entering the playing field of a sports game, usually without permission.
- After the unexpected goal, some excited fans ran onto the pitch.
- Security had to stop the protestors who ran onto the pitch during the final.

**break seats and lights** （损坏座位和灯光）
Describes the action of damaging property, specifically seats and lights, often in a public place like a stadium, due to destructive behavior.
- During the riots, hooligans were seen to break seats and lights in the arena.
- After the team lost, some angry fans began to break seats and lights in frustration.

**looking at videos** （查看视频）
The action of watching video recordings, often for the purpose of investigation or information gathering.
- The police are looking at videos to identify the person responsible for the theft.
- The coach was looking at videos of the last game to analyze their strategy.
