## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**in the final** （在决赛中）
This phrase refers to the last game of a competition.
- Spain won the game in the final.
- It was an exciting match in the final.

**extra time** （加时赛）
This refers to additional time played in a match when the score is tied at the end of the regular time.
- The game went into extra time.
- They scored the winning goal in extra time.

**play very well** （表现非常好）
This describes someone or a team performing excellently in a game or sport.
- The team played very well in the championship.
- She played very well and won the match.

**as a team** （作为一个团队）
This emphasizes cooperation and collective effort among members of a group.
- They succeeded because they worked well as a team.
- As a team, we can achieve anything.
