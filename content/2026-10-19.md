## 📖 文章 / Article

16-10-2026 15:00

A new investigation shows how Russian billionaire Roman Abramovich hid his great wealth to avoid paying taxes.

Secret documents show that companies in Cyprus wrote fake and backdated papers for him. These papers helped hide over 600 million dollars worth of luxury items, including yachts, private planes, and expensive houses. The advisers also changed bank records, so Abramovich wouldn’t have to pay millions in taxes.

Today, Abramovich faces strict sanctions in many countries because of his close relationship with Russian President Vladimir Putin. He can no longer do business in Britain, and his large superyachts are now parked in Turkey. Experts say changing official financial records to avoid taxes is against the law. Abramovich denies doing anything wrong and says he only followed advice from his financial team.

Difficult words: avoid (to stay away from something or prevent something bad from happening), backdate (to put an earlier date on an official document), advice (an opinion given to help someone decide what to do).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**hide his great wealth** （隐藏他的巨额财富）
Used to describe the action of concealing a large amount of money or valuable assets, often for illegal or secretive reasons. Register: Formal/Informal, depending on context.
- The politician was accused of trying to hide his great wealth from the public.

**avoid paying taxes** （逃税）
To illegally prevent yourself from giving money to the government when it is required by law. Register: Formal, often used in legal or financial contexts.
- The company was fined for trying to avoid paying taxes on its profits.
- It is illegal to avoid paying taxes by not declaring income.

**against the law** （违法的）
Something that is not permitted by the rules or regulations of a legal system. Register: Formal/Informal.
- Speeding is against the law and can result in a fine.
- Selling counterfeit goods is definitely against the law.

**follows advice** （遵从建议）
To act according to the recommendations or suggestions given by someone else. Register: General.
- She always follows advice from her doctor regarding her health.
- He decided to follow advice from his mentor and change his career path.
