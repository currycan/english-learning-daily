## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**special because** （特别，因为）
Used to explain the reason why something is unusual or important.
- This phone is special because it has a great camera.
- The concert was special because it was the band's last performance.

**shows the famous** （展示了著名的）
Used when something displays or reveals a well-known item, person, or place.
- The museum exhibit shows the famous Mona Lisa painting.
- The travel guide shows the famous landmarks in Rome.

**looks like** （看起来像）
Used to describe the appearance of something by comparing it to something else. Common in everyday conversation.
- The sky looks like it will rain soon.
- This building looks like a castle.

**put light on it** （打光在上面）
To direct light onto an object, often to see it better or to reveal something hidden.
- When you put light on it, the hidden message appears.
- She put light on it, and the dusty shelves were visible.
