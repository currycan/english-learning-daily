## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**changes our brain** （改变我们的大脑）
This phrase describes how an activity or experience can affect the way our brain works. It's a neutral observation.
- Listening to music regularly changes our brain and improves memory.
- Learning a new skill often changes our brain by creating new connections.

**think clearly** （清晰地思考）
This means to think in a way that is organized, logical, and easy to understand. It's often used when talking about problem-solving or making decisions.
- It's important to eat well to think clearly and concentrate.
- After a good night's sleep, she could finally think clearly about the problem.

**a lot** （很多）
This is a common way to say 'many' or 'much'. It's informal and used in everyday conversation.
- She reads a lot and enjoys learning new things.
- He practiced the piano a lot before the concert.
