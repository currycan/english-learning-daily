In 79 AD Mount Vesuvius erupts. The ash covers the Herculaneum scrolls.

Now, scientists find the scrolls. They are using smart technology to read them. They find out about Plato. Plato is a famous philosopher. He dies when he is 81 years old. The scrolls tell us about his life. Now, we also know more about where his body is.

Professor Graziano Ranocchia from an Italian university leads the team. They want to use the same technology to read other scrolls, too. Finding the scrolls is very important. It tells us a lot about Plato and history.

Difficult words: ash (the grey powder which we get when something burns), scroll (a roll of paper for writing on), history (things which happen in the past).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**find out about** （找出关于，了解）
To discover or learn information about something or someone. Used in general conversation and writing.
- Scientists are trying to find out about the ancient civilization.
- I need to find out about the new rules for the library.

**famous philosopher** （著名哲学家）
Refers to a philosopher who is widely known and recognized. Used in historical and academic contexts.
- Socrates is a famous philosopher from ancient Greece.
- The article discussed the ideas of a famous philosopher from the Renaissance.

**smart technology** （智能技术）
Advanced or innovative technological methods or tools. Used in discussions about modern science and innovation.
- This factory uses smart technology to improve efficiency.
- Many people are interested in the latest developments in smart technology.
