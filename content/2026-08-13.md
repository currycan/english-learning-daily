The European Union has a new digital passport system. It uses photos of faces and fingerprints for people from non-EU countries. Travelers go to a machine first.

Different airports use different machines, so it is hard to solve problems. Today, travelers cannot do any checks on their phones before they travel. Greece can still use old passport stamps. Airport leaders want to stop the new system during the summer months to help people travel fast.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**digital passport system** （数字护照系统）
This refers to an electronic system used for travel identification, replacing traditional paper passports with digital information.
- The new digital passport system aims to speed up border checks.
- Many countries are adopting digital passport systems for security.

**non-EU countries** （非欧盟国家）
Countries that are not members of the European Union. This phrase is used to specify people or things from outside the EU.
- People from non-EU countries may face different entry requirements.
- The airport introduced new procedures for travelers from non-EU countries.

**border officer** （边境官员）
An official responsible for controlling people and goods entering or leaving a country at a border crossing.
- The traveler presented their documents to the border officer.
- A border officer checked everyone's passport before they entered the country.

**long lines** （长队）
A situation where many people are waiting one after another, often causing delays.
- We had to wait in long lines at the security check.
- During holidays, you can expect long lines at popular tourist attractions.
