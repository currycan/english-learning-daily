## 📖 文章 / Article

03-07-2026 15:00

Plastic period products are very bad for the natural world. They are fifth among single-use plastics on European beaches, which is even higher than plastic cups and straws.

The European Parliament said that it was necessary to look for alternatives. One such alternative involves biopolymer fibers which come from algae. It is naturally white or colorless, therefore it is not necessary to bleach it. These fibers are used to make tampons, but their climate impact depends on how farmers grow algae. More companies are looking for a solution which will be more friendly to the natural world but safe for women to use.

Difficult words: single-use (something what you use only once), alternative (a different option), biopolymer (a polymer which comes from living organisms), bleach (to make something white).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**bad for the natural world** （对自然界有害）
Used to describe something that causes harm or damage to the environment.
- Pollution from factories can be very bad for the natural world.
- Using too much plastic is bad for the natural world.

**look for alternatives** （寻找替代品）
To search for other options or choices when the current one is not suitable or desirable.
- The company decided to look for alternatives to fossil fuels.
- We need to look for alternatives to this outdated system.

**friendly to the natural world** （对自然界友好）
Describing products or actions that do not harm the environment.
- Many consumers prefer products that are friendly to the natural world.
- Sustainable farming practices are friendly to the natural world.
