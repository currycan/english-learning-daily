## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**AI race** （人工智能竞赛）
Used to describe competition between countries or companies to develop and lead in artificial intelligence technology. It has a competitive and ambitious tone.
- The government is investing heavily in the AI race.
- Which country will win the AI race in the next decade?

**make AI safe** （使人工智能安全）
Refers to the process of ensuring that artificial intelligence systems do not cause harm and are controlled responsibly. It's about safety and security.
- The developers are working hard to make AI safe for public use.
- Ethical guidelines are crucial to make AI safe.

**safety rules** （安全规则）
Regulations or guidelines designed to prevent harm or accidents. This phrase is formal and relates to protection and security.
- The factory implemented new safety rules for all workers.
- We need clear safety rules for autonomous vehicles.

**keep everyone safe** （保护所有人安全）
To protect all people from danger or harm. This is a general and important objective.
- The new public health measures are designed to keep everyone safe.
- The firefighters worked tirelessly to keep everyone safe during the fire.
