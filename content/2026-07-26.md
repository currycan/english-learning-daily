Many people learn English for years, but they still can’t speak it well.

Many students want a better way to learn English. These five simple rules can help.

One, learn new words in a story. Don’t learn only single words. A story helps you remember new words.

Two, read and listen to the same words again and again. Stop only when you know them well.

Three, speak English as much as you can. Reading and listening are good, but you learn more when you speak.

Four, use lessons for your level. If the lessons are very easy or very difficult, you learn very little.

Finally, have a plan. Follow your plan every day.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**learn English** （学习英语）
The action of acquiring knowledge and skills in the English language. Common in educational contexts.
- Many people want to learn English for their careers.

**speak it well** （说得好）
To be able to communicate effectively and fluently in a language. Used when describing a person's language ability.
- After living abroad for five years, she can now speak it well.
- He studied hard to speak it well before his trip.

**as much as you can** （尽你所能）
To do something to the greatest extent possible. Encouraging maximum effort.

- Students should practice speaking English as much as they can.

To create or possess a set of steps or intentions for achieving a goal. Often used in contexts of organization and strategy.
