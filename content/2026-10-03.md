## 📖 文章 / Article

02-10-2026 15:00

Basmati rice is one of the world’s most valuable rice varieties, and sells for up to twice the price of standard white rice. Both India and Pakistan grow this crop in the historical region of Punjab, which is now divided by an international border.

For years, both nations have been locked in a legal dispute over the exclusive right to the Basmati name in the EU. Receiving Protected Geographical Indication (PGI) status would grant a valuable market monopoly, but neither country has been able to submit a joint application due to a lack of political cooperation. Although similar disputes, such as Halloumi cheese in divided Cyprus, were resolved through joint EU frameworks, negotiations between India and Pakistan have completely stalled.

Without an agreement or EU protection, approximately 90% of rice sold globally under the name Basmati comes from other regions. Experts warn that if the EU delays its decision further it may destroy its protection completely.

Difficult words: exclusive right (the official permission given to only one person or group to do or sell something), monopoly (complete control of a market or product by one single company or country), stall (to stop moving forward or making progress, often unexpectedly).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**locked in a legal dispute** （陷入法律纠纷）
This phrase means that two or more parties are in a serious disagreement that is being handled through legal channels. It suggests a prolonged and difficult situation.
- The companies have been locked in a legal dispute over patent infringement for months.
- For years, the two neighbors were locked in a legal dispute over their property line.

**Protected Geographical Indication (PGI)** （地理标志保护）
This is a specific type of protection status granted by the EU to food products that have a specific geographical origin and whose quality or reputation is due to that origin. It helps protect producers from unfair competition.
- The cheese is seeking Protected Geographical Indication status to preserve its traditional production methods.
- Many regional specialties rely on PGI to maintain their unique identity and value.

**negotiations have completely stalled** （谈判完全陷入僵局）
This means that talks or discussions between parties have stopped and are not making any progress. It suggests a complete lack of movement towards an agreement.
- Despite efforts from mediators, the negotiations have completely stalled.
- After weeks of discussions, the negotiations have completely stalled, with neither side willing to compromise.

**sells for up to** （售价高达）
This indicates the maximum price at which something is sold. It is often used to highlight a significant or premium price point.
- Rare collectibles can sell for up to thousands of dollars.
- This limited edition watch sells for up to twice the price of the standard model.
