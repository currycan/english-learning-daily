## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**around the Earth** （在地球周围）
This phrase describes a location or position that encircles or is in the vicinity of the planet Earth. It's a common geographical or orbital description.
- There is a lot of trash around the Earth.
- Many satellites orbit around the Earth to provide communication services.

**move very fast** （移动得非常快）
This describes rapid motion. It's used to indicate speed and urgency, often in a physical sense.
- The race car was designed to move very fast.
- News of the event started to move very fast through social media.

**clean space** （清理太空）
This refers to the act of removing unwanted or harmful objects from outer space to make it safer.
- Scientists want to clean space of debris.
- Organizations are working on technologies to clean space.

**take care of** （照顾, 照料, 负责）
This means to be responsible for something or someone, or to protect and preserve it.
- People must take care of space.
- It's important to take care of your health by eating well.
