## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**feel better** （感觉好些）
To experience an improvement in health or mood. Used informally to describe emotional or physical improvement.
- After a good rest, she started to feel better.
- Talking to a friend can help you feel better when you're sad.

**special training** （特殊训练）
Particular or specific instruction and practice to teach someone or an animal a skill or a behavior.
- The police dogs receive special training for search and rescue missions.
- She is undergoing special training to become a concert pianist.

**in the future** （将来）
At a time yet to come. Used to talk about plans or possibilities that will happen later.
- We hope to travel the world in the future.
- The company is planning to expand its services in the future.
