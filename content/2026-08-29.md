Five teenagers died in Ireland after driving a stolen car the wrong way on a busy highway.

Their car crashed directly into another vehicle carrying a family to the airport. Four family members were seriously injured, and two women are still fighting for their lives in the hospital.

Officials are now contacting TikTok to ensure the company takes down these dangerous videos. TikTok claimed it is actively monitoring the platform and deleting accounts that break its rules against illegal actions.

Used to describe moving or going in a direction opposite to the correct or intended one, often leading to danger on roads.

- The signs clearly indicated that the road was one-way, but the driver went the wrong way.

Websites and applications that enable users to create and share content or to participate in social networking. It is a very common term in modern communication.

**dangerous driving** （危险驾驶）
Refers to operating a vehicle in a way that puts oneself or others at risk of harm. This can include speeding, reckless maneuvers, or driving under the influence.
- The police are cracking down on dangerous driving in residential areas.
