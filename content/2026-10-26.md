The ongoing war with Iran is the main topic in the upcoming Israeli elections. Most Israeli voters want attacks against Iran to continue. Opposition parties strongly criticize Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu for not winning.

Opposition leaders warn that Iran still holds dangerous amounts of uranium which could quickly be turned into nuclear weapons. Other politicians propose bombing Iran’s energy facilities to destroy its economy and overthrow the government.

Although politicians agree that Iran is an enemy, they fear Netanyahu may restart military strikes right before election day to get votes or delay the election process. They argue that winning the election is the priority, while future military steps should wait until a new leader is in place.

Difficult words: upcoming (happening in the near future), overthrow (to remove a leader of government by force), priority (something more important than other things).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

The most important subject or issue being discussed or considered. Used in general and news contexts.

- Environmental protection is a main topic for many political campaigns.

**opposition parties** （反对党）
Political groups that are not in power and disagree with the government's policies. Used in political and news contexts.
- The opposition parties have called for a vote of no confidence.
- In a two-party system, the opposition parties play a vital role in holding the government accountable.

**nuclear weapons** （核武器）
Weapons that use nuclear energy to cause destruction. Used in discussions about war, politics, and international security.
- The treaty aims to prevent the proliferation of nuclear weapons.
- The threat of nuclear weapons has shaped global politics for decades.

**military strikes** （军事打击）
Attacks carried out by a country's armed forces. Used in news and discussions about conflicts and warfare.

- International observers condemned the recent military strikes.
