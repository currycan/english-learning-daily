Weather experts say that people must be careful. When it rains in the canyon, hikers must run up to high ground to stay safe.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**a lot of** （许多）
Used to express a large quantity or amount of something. Common in everyday English.
- A lot of people enjoyed the festival.
- She has a lot of homework to do.

**makes the flood worse** （使洪水变得更糟）
Describes a factor that increases the severity or negative impact of a flood. Used when explaining causes of disaster.
- Heavy rainfall makes the flood worse.
- Poor drainage systems make the flood worse.

**stay safe** （保持安全）
To remain unharmed and protected from danger. Essential advice in dangerous situations.
- During the storm, it's important to stay safe indoors.
- Always wear a helmet to stay safe when cycling.

**run up to high ground** （跑到高处）
To quickly move to a higher elevation as a safety measure, especially during floods or rising water. Urgent instruction.
- When you hear the alarm, run up to high ground immediately.
- The villagers were told to run up to high ground before the water reached them.
