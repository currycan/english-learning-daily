## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**say no to** （拒绝，反对）
Used to express disagreement or refusal of something proposed or suggested. Register: Neutral.
- The committee decided to say no to the new proposal.
- Many citizens say no to increased taxes.

**vote against** （投票反对）
To express disapproval of something or someone by voting. Register: Neutral.
- The members voted against the amendment.
- She plans to vote against the new policy.

**stay friends with** （与...保持朋友关系）
To continue a friendly relationship with someone. Register: Informal to Neutral.
- It's important to stay friends with your neighbors.
- They managed to stay friends with each other after the breakup.

**think about** （考虑）
To consider something or someone; to reflect on a matter. Register: Neutral.
- We need to think about the consequences of this decision.
- She often thinks about her future career.
