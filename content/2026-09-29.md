28-09-2026 15:00

Many people in London do not want to buy flats today. In the past, people wait in lines to buy them, but now many flats stay empty for months.

Buying a flat is very expensive. It is expensive to build a flat. Also, banks want a lot more money. Many owners are sad. They cannot sell their homes. Some flats have broken gas pipes or dangerous walls from old fires. Owners do not fix the problems quickly.

Because of these problems, many young people cannot move to bigger homes. The government is trying to make buying homes cheaper and safer for everyone.

Difficult words: empty (with nothing or nobody inside), owner (a person or organization who something belongs to), pipe (a long tube to carry water or gas to homes).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**in the past** （过去）
Refers to a time before the present. Used to contrast with the current situation.
- In the past, people read books printed on paper.
- In the past, communication was much slower.

**stay empty** （保持空置）
Describes a place or object that is not occupied or used by anyone for a period of time.
- Many hotel rooms will stay empty during the off-season.
- The apartment has been vacant for months and will likely stay empty longer.

**sad** （伤心，难过）
Feeling or showing unhappiness. In this context, it describes the emotional state of the owners.
- She was sad when her pet got lost.
- He felt sad because he couldn't achieve his goals.

**dangerous walls** （危险的墙壁）
Walls that are not safe and could cause harm or damage.
- The old building had dangerous walls that needed to be repaired.
- After the earthquake, many houses had dangerous walls.

## ❓ 理解 / Comprehension

**Q: Why are many flats in London staying empty now?**
**A (EN):** Many flats are staying empty because buying them is very expensive, it's costly to build them, and banks demand more money. Also, owners are unable to sell their homes due to problems like broken pipes or unsafe walls that are not quickly fixed.
**A (中):** 许多伦敦的公寓现在空置着，因为购买公寓的成本非常高，建造公寓的费用也很昂贵，而且银行要求更多的钱。此外，由于管道破裂或旧火灾造成的墙壁不安全等问题没有及时修复，业主无法出售他们的房屋。

**Q: What is the government doing to help people buy homes?**
**A (EN):** The government is trying to make buying homes cheaper and safer for everyone.
**A (中):** 政府正试图让购房对每个人来说都更便宜、更安全。

**Q: What might be the consequence for young people if flats remain empty and difficult to sell?**
**A (EN):** If flats remain empty and difficult to sell, young people cannot move to bigger homes. This suggests that the housing market is not functioning well, making it harder for younger generations to find suitable accommodation as their needs change.
**A (中):** 如果公寓保持空置且难以出售，年轻人就无法搬入更大的住房。这表明房地产市场运行不畅，使得年轻一代在需求变化时更难找到合适的住所。
