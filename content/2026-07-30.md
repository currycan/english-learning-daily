## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**having problems** （遇到麻烦）
Used to describe a situation where something is not working well or facing difficulties. It's a common and direct way to express trouble.
- The car is having problems and won't start.
- Many businesses are having problems because of the new regulations.

**pay more for** （为...支付更多）
To spend a larger amount of money on something than before. Often used when prices increase.
- We have to pay more for electricity this month.
- She decided to pay more for a better quality product.

**bad news** （坏消息）
Information that is unwelcome or indicates a negative situation. It's a straightforward way to convey something unfavorable.
- The team lost the match, which is bad news for the fans.
- He heard some bad news about his job application.

**need money from** （需要来自...的钱）
To require financial support or donations from a particular source or group of people.
- The charity will need money from the public to continue its work.
- The project needs money from investors to get started.
