## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**have a big meal together** （一起吃大餐）
This phrase refers to sharing a formal or large meal with others, often in a social or celebratory context.
- The family will have a big meal together to celebrate the holiday.
- After the meeting, the team decided to have a big meal together.

**friendly words** （友好的话）
This refers to speaking in a kind, polite, and positive way, often to create a good atmosphere or relationship.
- Even though they disagreed on some points, they exchanged friendly words at the end.
- The neighbors always greet each other with friendly words.

**problems to solve** （需要解决的问题）
This indicates that there are issues or difficulties that need to be addressed or resolved. It is a common collocation in discussions about challenges.
- The new manager has many problems to solve in the department.
- Climate change is one of the biggest problems to solve in our lifetime.

**friendly gift** （友好的礼物）
A gift given as a sign of friendship or goodwill between individuals, groups, or countries.
- The company offered a small discount as a friendly gift to new customers.
- The donation was seen as a friendly gift to the community center.
