Twin sisters Morgan and Lisa move into the same kind of house. Morgan buys her home. Lisa rents hers.

Morgan must pay a lot of money in the beginning. She repairs the house when things break. After 30 years, Morgan pays off her home loan, and her house becomes worth a lot of money. She buys the house for 400,000 dollars. After 30 years, she can sell it for 1.3 million. Lisa pays rent every month. She does not pay for repairs. She saves money. She has a retirement account. After 30 years, she pays 900,000 dollars for rent. But she has 500,000 in her retirement account.

In the end, Morgan makes more money by buying. But Lisa is free to move. She can spend on hobbies, not a house.

Difficult words: twin sister (a girl who is born at the same time to the same mother as another child), rent (the money which you pay when you live in someone’s house or flat), retirement account (an account where you save money for the time after finishing work).

## 📚 词汇 / Vocabulary

**twin** (adjective) /twɪn/ （双胞胎的）— Two children born to the same mother at the same time.
> "Twin sisters Morgan and Lisa move into the same kind of house."

**rent** (verb) /rɛnt/ （租）— To pay money to use a place or an item for a period of time.
> "Lisa rents hers."

**repairs** (noun) /rɪˈpɛrz/ （维修）— Things that are done to fix something that is broken or damaged.
> "She repairs the house when things break."

**loan** (noun) /loʊn/ （贷款）— An amount of money that you borrow from a bank or other company and must pay back later, usually with interest.
> "After 30 years, Morgan pays off her home loan, and her house becomes worth a lot of money."

**retirement** (noun) /rɪˈtaɪərmənt/ （退休）— The period in your life after you have stopped working.
> "She has a retirement account."

**account** (noun) /əˈkaʊnt/ （账户）— A record of money that is paid into and taken out of a bank or organization.
> "She has a retirement account."

**free** (adjective) /fri/ （自由的）— Having the ability or permission to do what you want.
> "But Lisa is free to move."

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**pays off her home loan** （还清房屋贷款）
This phrase means to finish paying all the money that was borrowed to buy a house.
- They worked hard for 20 years to pay off their home loan.
- Once you pay off your home loan, the house is completely yours.

**saves money** （省钱，储蓄）
This means to keep money instead of spending it, usually for future use.
- By cooking at home, she saves money on eating out.
- He tries to save money every month for his vacation.

**in the end** （最后，最终）
This phrase means after a long time or process; eventually.
- We faced many challenges, but in the end, we succeeded.
- In the end, they decided to buy the house.

**free to move** （可以自由搬家）
This means having the ability or permission to relocate easily, without strong obligations or ties to a specific place.
- As a young professional, she was free to move for new job opportunities.
- Because he didn't own a house, he was free to move to a different city.

**Q: What does the article suggest is a disadvantage of buying a house?**

**Q: Inferential Question: Considering both options, which person's financial situation might be more flexible in the very long term, even if one initially gains more money?**

**A (中):** 丽莎的财务状况在非常长远的未来可能更灵活。虽然摩根从她房产的升值中总体上赚到了更多的钱，但丽莎能够‘自由搬家’并‘花钱在爱好上，而不是房子上’，这表明她的生活方式有较少的固定财务负担，并且可能从她的储蓄中获得更多的即时可支配收入，从而具有更大的适应性。
