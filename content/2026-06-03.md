## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**blocking the roads** （阻塞道路）
This phrase is used to describe the action of preventing traffic or people from using a road, often as a form of protest.
- Protesters were blocking the roads to the city center.
- They are blocking the roads to demand better working conditions.

**do not have food, gas, or medicine** （没有食物、汽油或药品）
This describes a severe shortage of essential supplies, indicating a crisis or a significant disruption.
- After the storm, many areas did not have food, gas, or medicine.
- The blockade meant the town did not have food, gas, or medicine.

**wants to send soldiers** （想要派士兵）
This phrase indicates a government's intention to use military force, often to control a situation or restore order.
- The government wants to send soldiers to the border.
- The leader wants to send soldiers to stop the unrest.
