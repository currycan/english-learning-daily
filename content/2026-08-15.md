Two famous places in Latin America are in big danger today.

Machu Picchu in Peru has too many tourists every year. Many people walk on old stones and damage the site. Bad weather and heavy rain also cause big problems. The government now limits how many people can visit each day. In Honduras, people cut down big trees in a famous rainforest. Illegal farms, gold mining, and criminals destroy the forest and harm wild animals.

Local people try to save their land, but it is dangerous. Experts say that countries must work together now to help save these special nature sites.

Difficult words: site (a specific place where something is located), limit (the maximum number or amount which is still possible), mining (digging into the ground to get metals).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**in big danger** （处于巨大的危险中）
Used to describe a serious or severe risk of harm or disaster. It's a common and straightforward way to express a significant threat.
- The old bridge is in big danger of collapsing during the storm.
- Without immediate action, many animal species are in big danger of extinction.

**cut down** （砍倒）
To fell a tree or trees. This is a common phrasal verb related to deforestation or removing trees from an area.
- They had to cut down the diseased tree to prevent it from falling on the house.
- The company was fined for illegally cutting down trees in the protected forest.

**work together** （一起工作）
To collaborate or cooperate on a task or goal. It emphasizes joint effort and shared responsibility.
- To finish the project on time, the team decided to work together closely.
- Governments and citizens need to work together to solve environmental problems.
