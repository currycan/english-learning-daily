## 📖 文章 / Article

02-09-2026 15:00

In 1848, construction worker Phineas Gage has a bad accident. An iron rod goes through his head and hurts the front part of his brain. Gage does not die, but his personality changes. He is not kind and organized anymore.

This event is important for doctors. It shows that the brain and personality are connected to some parts of the brain. Gage later finds work in Chile. His job is repetitive. For Gage, it is a therapy. His brain must learn to work differently.  Doctors learn that the brain can change.

Gage’s accident starts a new field of neuroscience. It teaches us that the brain can adapt and recover from serious injuries.

Difficult words: rod (a thin, straight, solid bar), brain (the organ inside the head), personality (how you act and what you think), repetitive (when something happens again and again).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**goes through his head** （穿过他的头）
This describes a physical penetration of an object into the head. It's a direct and literal description of an event.
- The bullet goes through his head and he survives.
- The sharp object goes through his head, causing a severe injury.

**changes his personality** （改变了他的个性）
This indicates a significant alteration in how someone behaves, thinks, and feels. It suggests a deep, internal shift.
- The traumatic experience changed his personality drastically.
- After moving to a new city, her personality changed in unexpected ways.

**field of neuroscience** （神经科学领域）
This refers to a specific area of scientific study focused on the brain and nervous system. It's a formal term used in academic and scientific contexts.
- She decided to pursue a career in the field of neuroscience.
- Many breakthroughs in the field of neuroscience have been made recently.

**recover from serious injuries** （从重伤中恢复）
This describes the process of healing and regaining health after a severe physical harm. It implies a long and possibly difficult process.
- It will take him a long time to recover from his serious injuries.
- The hospital specializes in helping patients recover from serious injuries.
