## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**very strong** （非常强大）
Used to emphasize the power or capability of something, often used for technology.
- The new phone has a very strong battery that lasts for days.
- This novel is very strong in its character development.

**computer code** （计算机代码）
The set of instructions that computers follow to perform tasks. It's a technical term.
- The programmer spent hours debugging the computer code.
- Learning computer code is essential for many tech jobs.

**find mistakes** （找出错误）
To discover errors or problems in something, often used in the context of work, programs, or writing.
- The editor's job is to find mistakes in the manuscript.
- This software update is designed to find mistakes in your data entry.

**make rules** （制定规则）
To create regulations or guidelines that people or systems must follow.  Often used in a formal or governmental context.
- The government will make rules for online safety.
- It's important to make rules for children to follow at home.
