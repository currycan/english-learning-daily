Learning a new language can be difficult, especially if you only remember funny, random textbook phrases. To help you improve, there are five effective hacks.

First, skip complicated grammar rules at the beginning and learn the 50 most common phrases. Make them personal; focus on words you will actually use in daily life. Second, listen to the language as much as possible through YouTube, radio, or audiobooks, preferably just above your current level. This tunes your ear and helps with pattern recognition. Third, practice talking to yourself, your pets, or plants to get comfortable. Fourth, relax on the sofa and watch TV shows, movies, or children’s programs, and follow social media accounts in your target language to learn real expressions. Fifth, practice with an AI chatbot; it is like a 24/7 tutor that provides a safe space to make mistakes without embarrassment.

Remember that language is social, and real conversations with humans are the most important part of becoming fluent.

Difficult words: hack (a clever tip, trick, or efficient way to do something more easily), pattern recognition (the ability of the brain to see a design or a regular arrangement of words and sounds that repeat), embarrassment (feeling ashamed, shy, or uncomfortable because of a mistake or a silly situation).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**improve your language skills** （提高你的语言能力）
A common phrase used to describe the process of getting better at speaking, listening, reading, or writing a language. It's a general and positive term.
- To improve your language skills, you should practice daily.
- This app is designed to help you improve your language skills quickly.

**as much as possible** （尽可能地）
Used to express that something should be done to the greatest extent or degree that is achievable. It's a very common and versatile phrase.
- Try to listen to native speakers as much as possible.
- We need to save money as much as possible for our trip.

**get comfortable** （变得自在，习惯）
Means to feel relaxed and at ease in a situation, or to become used to something new. It's often used when learning a new skill or adapting to a new environment.
- Talking to yourself can help you get comfortable with new words.
- It takes time to get comfortable with public speaking.

**target language** （目标语言）
The language that someone is trying to learn. It's a specific term used in language learning contexts.
- Watching movies in your target language is a great way to learn.
- She has been studying her target language for three years.
