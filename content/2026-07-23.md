## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**find a job** （找到一份工作）
Used to describe the act of getting employment. Common and neutral register.
- It is becoming harder for people to find a job after graduation.
- She is actively looking to find a job in the tech industry.

**leave their country** （离开他们的国家）
Used when people decide to move away from their homeland, often to live somewhere else. Neutral register.
- Many people choose to leave their country for better opportunities.
- He missed his family after he had to leave his country for work.

**speak freely** （自由地说话）
To express your opinions and ideas without fear of punishment or censorship. Often used in contexts related to freedom of speech.
- In a democracy, citizens should be able to speak freely about political issues.
- The new policy allows employees to speak freely about their concerns.

**moving to France** （搬到法国）
The act of changing one's residence and relocating to France. Can refer to temporary or permanent moves. Neutral register.
- They are moving to France next month for a new business venture.
- Many international students are moving to France to experience its culture.
