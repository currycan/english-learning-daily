07-08-2026 15:00

A New Year’s resolution is a promise which you make to yourself. You say that you will start doing something good or stop doing something bad on 1 January.

Many people have problems with New Year’s resolutions. They often fail. Professor Scot Lear says that our goals must be SMART – Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Timely. An example is exercising three times a week for 30 minutes for three months. If there are problems, it is okay. Things are not always as we want.

Scot says that we must be kind to ourselves, learn from our mistakes, and not stop.

Difficult words: specific (clear), measurable (possible to measure), achievable (possible to do), relevant (useful, related to you).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**make a promise** （许下诺言）
To commit to doing something or ensuring something happens. Commonly used in both formal and informal contexts.
- I made a promise to myself to read more books this year.
- The politician made a promise to the voters during the campaign.

**problems with** （在...方面有问题）
Used to describe difficulties or issues encountered with a particular thing or situation. It's a common and direct way to express challenges.
- Many people have problems with New Year’s resolutions.
- She's been having problems with her car lately.

**learn from** （从...中学习）
To gain knowledge or skill from an experience, especially a negative one. This phrase emphasizes the positive outcome of a lesson learned.
- It's important to learn from our mistakes.
- We need to learn from history to avoid repeating past errors.

## ❓ 理解 / Comprehension

**Q: What is a New Year's resolution?**
**A (EN):** A New Year's resolution is a promise you make to yourself to start doing something good or stop doing something bad on January 1st.
**A (中):** 新年决心是你对自己许下的承诺，即在1月1日开始做某件好事或停止做某件坏事。

**Q: According to Professor Scot Lear, what qualities should goals have?**
**A (EN):** According to Professor Scot Lear, goals must be SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Timely.
**A (中):** 根据Scot Lear教授的说法，目标必须是SMART的：具体的、可衡量的、可实现的、相关的和及时的。

**Q: Why might someone's New Year's resolution fail, even if they try their best?**
**A (EN):** A New Year's resolution might fail because things don't always go as planned or as we want them to. It's important to be kind to ourselves, learn from any mistakes, and not give up.
**A (中):** 新年决心可能失败，是因为事情并不总是如计划或我们所愿。重要的是善待自己，从任何错误中学习，并且不放弃。
