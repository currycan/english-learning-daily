An agency in the UK reports that children are using electronic devices more and more. Some 3 to 4-year-old children use a tablet over an hour a day.

Some 12 to 15-year-old children spend many hours a week on the Internet, and many use their phones in bed.

A psychologist said that parents need to set rules when and where their children can use their devices. One mother said she often argues with her children about it.

There is no ideal limit to using electronic devices, but the advice is to keep it low.

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**more and more** （越来越多）
Used to describe an increase or progression. Common in everyday conversation and informal writing.
- More and more people are choosing to work from home.
- The problem is becoming more and more serious.

**spend hours on** （花费（时间）在…上）
To use time doing a particular activity. Often implies a significant amount of time. Used in both formal and informal contexts.
- She likes to spend hours on her hobbies.
- Students often spend hours on homework each night.

**set rules** （制定规则）
To establish guidelines or instructions for behavior. Common in parenting, management, and organizational contexts.
- Parents need to set rules for their children's screen time.
- The company has set rules for employee conduct.

**keep it low** （保持低水平）
To maintain something at a small or reduced amount or level. Often used in relation to quantities, costs, or usage.
- We should try to keep our expenses low this month.
- The doctor advised him to keep his salt intake low.
