## 📖 文章 / Article

21-09-2026 15:00

The European Central Bank shows ten designs for new euro money. Bank leaders choose from 1,200 ideas. They pick two main topics.

The first topic shows famous people like Leonardo da Vinci, Marie Curie, and Beethoven. The second topic shows birds and rivers to help protect nature. Now, people who live in Europe can choose their favorite design. They can vote on the bank’s website.

After the vote, the bank will make the new paper money. People will start to use the new euro money at the end of 2026.

Difficult words: topic (the main idea), favorite (when many people like a person or thing), vote (to officially choose a person, idea, or plan).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**European Central Bank** （欧洲中央银行）
The official name of the bank responsible for monetary policy in the Eurozone. This is a formal term used in news and official contexts.
- The European Central Bank announced new interest rates yesterday.
- Residents can submit their design preferences to the European Central Bank.

**choose from** （从...中选择）
To select one or more things from a group of options. Used in many contexts, from shopping to making decisions.
- We have to choose from three different menu options.
- The committee will choose from the shortlisted candidates.

**protect nature** （保护自然）
To take care of the environment, animals, and plants. This is a common phrase used in discussions about conservation and the environment.
- Many organizations are working to protect nature.
- Our new policy is designed to protect nature in this region.

**at the end of** （在...的末尾/结束时）
Used to indicate the final part of a period of time. Common in everyday conversation and writing.
- Let's plan the party for the end of the month.
- The shop will close at the end of the day.
