## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**feel stuck** （感到停滞不前；感到进退两难）
Used to describe a situation where someone is unable to make progress or move forward, often in their personal or professional life. It implies a feeling of frustration.
- After graduating, she started to feel stuck in her job.
- Many artists feel stuck when they don't have inspiration.

**wait for the best time** （等待最佳时机）
To postpone taking action because one is looking for the most opportune moment, often leading to inaction.
- He's been waiting for the best time to ask for a raise.
- Don't wait for the best time; start now.

**do something now** （现在就做某事）
An imperative phrase encouraging immediate action rather than delay. It emphasizes urgency.
- The project deadline is tomorrow, so we need to do something now.
- If you want to succeed, you have to do something now, not later.

**a bad first try** （糟糕的第一次尝试）
Describes an initial attempt at something that is not successful or goes poorly. It acknowledges that beginnings can be difficult.
- My first attempt at baking a cake was a bad first try, but I learned a lot.
- Even a bad first try is better than not trying at all.
