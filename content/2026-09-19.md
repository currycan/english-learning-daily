## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**AI race** （人工智能竞赛）
Refers to the competition between countries or companies to develop and lead in artificial intelligence technology. Used in a competitive context.
- The country is investing heavily to win the global AI race.
- Many experts believe that the AI race is crucial for future economic growth.

**make AI safe** （使人工智能安全）
To ensure that artificial intelligence systems do not cause harm and are used in a responsible manner. This is a key concern in discussions about AI development.
- Researchers are working to develop algorithms that will make AI safe for public use.
- The company promised to implement strict measures to make AI safe.

**safety rules** （安全规则）
Regulations and guidelines designed to prevent harm and ensure well-being, particularly in the context of new or potentially dangerous technologies like AI.
- The government is considering new safety rules for autonomous vehicles.
- There is a strong debate about the need for more stringent safety rules in AI development.

**make money** （赚钱）
To earn income or profit from business activities. This is a common motivation for companies.
- Many startups are focused on innovative ways to make money.
- He left his job to start a business and make money.
