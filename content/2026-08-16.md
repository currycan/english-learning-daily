Two famous places in Latin America are in big danger today.

Machu Picchu in Peru has too many tourists every year. Many people walk on old stones and damage the site. Bad weather and heavy rain also cause big problems. The government now limits how many people can visit each day. In Honduras, people cut down big trees in a famous rainforest. Illegal farms, gold mining, and criminals destroy the forest and harm wild animals.

Local people try to save their land, but it is dangerous. Experts say that countries must work together now to help save these special nature sites.

Difficult words: site (a specific place where something is located), limit (the maximum number or amount which is still possible), mining (digging into the ground to get metals).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**in danger** （处于危险之中）
Used to describe a situation or place that is at risk of harm or damage. Common in news reports and discussions about safety.
- The ancient monument is in danger of collapsing.
- Many animal species are in danger due to habitat loss.

**cut down** （砍伐（树木））
To fell a tree or trees. Often used in contexts related to deforestation or logging.
- The company was accused of illegally cutting down trees in the national park.
- We need to stop cutting down forests to protect the environment.

**work together** （一起工作，合作）
To collaborate or cooperate on a task or goal. Emphasizes joint effort and shared responsibility. Used in both formal and informal contexts.
- The two departments will work together to solve the problem.
- If we work together, we can achieve anything.
