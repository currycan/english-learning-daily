## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**AI race** （人工智能竞赛）
Refers to the competition between countries or companies to develop and lead in artificial intelligence technology. It's a common term in discussions about technological advancement.
- Many countries are investing heavily to win the AI race.
- The article suggests that the US wants to dominate the AI race.

**tech leaders** （科技领袖）
Refers to the most important or influential people in the technology industry, such as CEOs of major tech companies or innovators.
- Tech leaders met to discuss the future of the internet.
- The opinions of tech leaders are often sought after.

**safety rules** （安全规则）
Regulations or guidelines designed to prevent harm or danger, especially in the context of new or powerful technologies.
- The government is proposing new safety rules for self-driving cars.
- Many people feel that more safety rules are needed for AI.

**make money** （赚钱）
A very common and straightforward phrase meaning to earn money, often implies profit or financial gain. Used in both formal and informal contexts.
- The company's main goal is to make money for its shareholders.
- He started a small business to try and make money.
