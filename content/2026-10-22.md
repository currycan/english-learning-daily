## 📖 文章 / Article

21-10-2026 07:00

Bangladesh is struggling with a measles outbreak which caused thousands of hospitalizations and over 1,100 deaths, mostly among young children.

The crisis started after political protests in 2025 disrupted routine healthcare and vaccination programs. Because fewer children received their shots, national immunity fell below safe levels. The government quickly organized an emergency vaccination campaign, but it missed millions of children due to bad planning, missing data, and a lack of outreach in rural villages. Many parents reported that health workers never visited their homes before their children fell sick.

Experts say this health emergency is a strong warning to other countries about the importance of keeping vaccination rates high. Without strong immunization programs, preventable diseases can quickly return and kill thousands of people.

Difficult words: measles (a dangerous disease which causes a fever and a red rash on the skin), hospitalization (taking someone to a hospital), outreach (providing services or medical help directly to people in local communities), preventable (able to be stopped before it happens).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**struggling with** （与...斗争；难以对付）
Used to describe a difficult situation or problem that someone is trying to overcome. Often used with abstract nouns like 'disease', 'poverty', or 'debt'.
- The company is struggling with low sales this quarter.
- Many families are struggling with the rising cost of living.

**fell below safe levels** （降至安全水平以下）
Indicates that a measurement or quantity has dropped to a point considered dangerous or not adequate for protection.
- The temperature in the room fell below safe levels for storing food.
- After the accident, the patient's blood pressure fell below safe levels.

**lack of outreach** （缺乏外展服务/沟通）
Indicates that efforts to connect with or provide services to a specific group of people, especially those in remote or underserved areas, are insufficient.
- The project failed due to a lack of outreach to the local community.
- There's a serious lack of outreach programs for homeless people in this city.

**strong warning** （严厉的警告）
A serious and significant signal that something bad could happen if certain actions are not taken or if a situation continues.
- The recent floods are a strong warning about the effects of climate change.
- The company received a strong warning from regulators about its business practices.
