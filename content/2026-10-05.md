## 📖 文章 / Article

02-10-2026 15:00

Basmati rice is one of the world’s most valuable rice varieties, and sells for up to twice the price of standard white rice. Both India and Pakistan grow this crop in the historical region of Punjab, which is now divided by an international border.

For years, both nations have been locked in a legal dispute over the exclusive right to the Basmati name in the EU. Receiving Protected Geographical Indication (PGI) status would grant a valuable market monopoly, but neither country has been able to submit a joint application due to a lack of political cooperation. Although similar disputes, such as Halloumi cheese in divided Cyprus, were resolved through joint EU frameworks, negotiations between India and Pakistan have completely stalled.

Without an agreement or EU protection, approximately 90% of rice sold globally under the name Basmati comes from other regions. Experts warn that if the EU delays its decision further it may destroy its protection completely.

Difficult words: exclusive right (the official permission given to only one person or group to do or sell something), monopoly (complete control of a market or product by one single company or country), stall (to stop moving forward or making progress, often unexpectedly).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**locked in a legal dispute** （陷入法律纠纷）
Describes a situation where two or more parties are involved in a serious disagreement that may lead to legal action. It implies a difficult and prolonged conflict.
- The two companies have been locked in a legal dispute over patent infringement for over a year.
- Neighboring countries are often locked in a legal dispute over border territories.

**Protected Geographical Indication (PGI) status** （受保护的地理标志（PGI）地位）
A certification from the European Union that protects the name of a food product originating from a specific geographical area, ensuring its quality and reputation.
- Parmigiano Reggiano cheese has PGI status, guaranteeing its authenticity.
- Gaining PGI status is crucial for the local wine industry's recognition.

**lack of political cooperation** （缺乏政治合作）
Refers to the absence of willingness or ability of political entities (like countries or parties) to work together towards a common goal.
- The project failed due to a significant lack of political cooperation between the different government departments.
- International efforts to combat climate change are often hindered by a lack of political cooperation.

**completely stalled** （完全停滞）
Indicates that progress has stopped entirely, often in a negotiation or a project, with no signs of moving forward.
- After the latest disagreement, the peace talks have completely stalled.
- Construction on the new library has completely stalled because of funding issues.
