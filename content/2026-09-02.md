## 📖 文章 / Article

01-09-2026 15:00

A new project in Austria is causing anger because workers are building a business park on the site of a former Nazi camp.

During World War II, about 400 women were kept at the Hirtenberg camp and forced to work in a factory. Today, developers are building a supermarket and large storage buildings on this land. Officials said there were not enough old buildings left to legally protect the site, but historians and activists feel sad and angry. They believe that places with dark history should be saved, not used for shopping and business.

The company building the park offered to pay for a small memorial. Even so, many people think that building over a concentration camp forgets the victims and hides important history.

Difficult words: historian (a person who studies, researches, and writes about the past), memorial (an object, structure, or event created to remember a person or important event), victim (a person who is harmed or killed as a result of a crime, accident, or war).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**causing anger** （引起愤怒）
Used to describe something that makes people feel very unhappy or displeased. It's a common way to express a negative reaction to an event or situation.
- The new policy is causing anger among the residents.
- His careless remarks were causing anger throughout the company.

**forced to work** （被迫工作）
This phrase describes a situation where someone has no choice but to perform labor, often under unpleasant or involuntary conditions. It implies a lack of freedom.
- During the war, many prisoners were forced to work in dangerous mines.
- She felt forced to work overtime every weekend.

**feel sad and angry** （感到悲伤和愤怒）
Expresses a strong emotional response, combining deep unhappiness with a sense of outrage or strong displeasure. It indicates a significant negative emotional impact.
- Many people feel sad and angry about the environmental destruction.
- The students feel sad and angry about the unfair grading system.

**building over** （在...上面建造）
This means constructing a building on top of something that already exists, potentially covering or replacing it. It can sometimes have negative connotations if what is being covered is historically significant.
- They are planning on building over the old car park.
- The community protested against building over the ancient ruins.
