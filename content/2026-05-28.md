## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**very strong** （非常强大）
Used to describe something that has a lot of power, ability, or influence. It's a common and direct way to express a high degree of power.
- The new software is very strong and can handle many tasks.
- She has a very strong opinion on the matter.

**find mistakes** （找到错误）
To discover or identify errors or problems in something. Often used in contexts of checking, reviewing, or debugging.
- The editor's job is to find mistakes in the text.
- Can you help me find mistakes in my code?

**make rules** （制定规则）
To create or establish guidelines or regulations. This is a common phrase used when discussing government, organizations, or any system that needs order.
- The school will make rules about wearing uniforms.
- It's important to make rules for online behavior.

**keep safe** （保持安全）
To protect someone or something from danger or harm, and ensure their well-being. It implies an ongoing action of maintaining security.
- We need to find ways to keep our children safe online.
- The police are working to keep the city safe.
