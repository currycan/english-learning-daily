## 📖 文章 / Article

09-10-2026 07:00

Many people worry that the internet is dying because AI is creating too much text and too many images. Today, automated bots make up more than 60% of web activity, and many new websites use AI to write articles.

This problem is called the dead internet theory. Tech companies know this is dangerous because AI tools copy work from real human writers. When people read quick AI summaries, they don’t click on the original websites, so human writers lose money and close their businesses.

In the future, AI will train on bad AI-generated content, spread mistakes, false news, and wrong health advice. To stay safe, readers should always check who wrote an article, look at real source websites, and support human journalists.

Difficult words: automated (done by machines or computers instead of people), human (about real people), AI-generated (created by AI).

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

Used to express concern or anxiety about a particular situation or possibility. Common in both spoken and written English.

- She began to worry that she would miss her train.

**make up** （构成, 组成）
To form or constitute a part of something. Often used for statistics or components of a whole. Informal to neutral register.
- Automated bots make up more than 60% of web activity.
- Plastic bottles make up a large part of the household waste.

**AI-generated content** （人工智能生成的内容）
Refers specifically to text, images, or other media created by artificial intelligence systems. A modern and technical term.

To avoid danger or harm. A common and practical phrase for giving advice.
