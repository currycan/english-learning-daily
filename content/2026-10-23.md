## 📖 文章 / Article

22-10-2026 15:00

Kenya’s biggest landfill is called Dandora. It is in Nairobi. It is one of the biggest landfills in the world.

Dandora is full of throwaway plastics. People use most of these plastics only once. Then they throw it away. Kenya is trying hard to be cleaner. Kenya wants a strict global rule on plastics. Countries are talking about it now. In 2017, Kenya makes tough laws about plastic bags. People can go to jail or pay a lot of money if they use plastic bags.

Now, a new law says that companies must handle their products’ waste, not consumers. A Kenyan milk company changes its bottles to clear ones. It is easier to recycle these bottles. People like this change. It shows that Kenyans care more about this problem than before.

Difficult words: landfill (a place where people put things which they do not need anymore, and it looks like a mountain), waste (things which people and companies throw away), consumer (a person who buys things).

## 📚 词汇 / Vocabulary

**landfill** (noun) /ˈlændfɪl/ （垃圾填埋场）— a place where large amounts of waste are buried under the ground
> "Kenya’s biggest landfill is called Dandora."

**throwaway** (adjective) /ˈθroʊəˌweɪ/ （一次性的；丢弃的）— designed to be used once and then thrown away
> "Dandora is full of throwaway plastics."

**strict** (adjective) /strɪkt/ （严格的）— following rules very carefully and having serious consequences if they are broken
> "Kenya wants a strict global rule on plastics."

**tough** (adjective) /tʌf/ （强硬的；严厉的）— severe or harsh, especially in rules or punishments
> "In 2017, Kenya makes tough laws about plastic bags."

**consumers** (noun) /kənˈsjuːmərz/ （消费者）— people who buy and use goods and services
> "Now, a new law says that companies must handle their products’ waste, not consumers."

**recycle** (verb) /riˈsaɪkl/ （回收利用）— to treat or process used materials so they can be used again
> "It is easier to recycle these bottles."

## 🔗 表达 / Chunking Expressions

**trying hard** （努力尝试）
Used to express a strong effort being made to achieve something. Common in both formal and informal contexts.
- She is trying hard to finish her homework before dinner.
- The company is trying hard to improve customer service.

**talk about** （谈论；讨论）
To discuss a particular subject with someone. Widely used in all registers.
- We need to talk about our plans for the weekend.
- Experts are talking about the impact of climate change.

**handle waste** （处理废物）
To manage or deal with waste materials. Often used in discussions about environmental responsibility and company policies.
- It is the responsibility of governments to handle waste effectively.
- Businesses are now legally required to handle their waste responsibly.

**care about** （关心；在乎）
To feel that something is important and to be concerned about it. Used in everyday conversation and more serious discussions.
- Many young people care about social justice issues.
- Do you care about what other people think?
